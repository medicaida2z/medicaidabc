Frequently Asked Questions

A. How to appeal a Pennsylvania Medicaid decision

If your Long-Term Care Medicaid application is denied or terminated, you have the right to file a written appeal with your local County Assistance Office (CAO) within 30 days of the mailing date on the notice. Request a fair hearing and ensure you maintain benefits pending appeal if filed within the required timeframe.

B. How to apply for PA Long-Term Care Medicaid

Applications can be submitted online via COMPASS PA (compass.state.pa.us), by mail, or in person at your county CAO. You must complete both financial forms and the medical assessment (Nursing Facility Clinically Eligible assessment) to establish clinical need.

C. How to request required documents from banks and businesses

When preparing for the mandatory 5-year look-back audit, request complete historical monthly statements directly from your bank branch managers or online portals. For closed accounts, life insurance policies, or annuities, submit formal written requests for "surrender value verification letters" and historical transaction logs.
